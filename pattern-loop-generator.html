Import

Transparent PNG (animated APNG too), GIF (animated or still), WebP or SVG. Animations use up to 24 frames.

Stamps

A stamp drops a handful of copies of the same sprite at one spot. Drag the preview to move the one you are editing.

Randomise varies each copy's size and turn. Speed turns the whole cluster about its middle, so the outer copies travel further than the inner ones; both it and Spin count whole turns per loop, which is what keeps the loop exact.

Arrangement

Each copy
Flip

Jitter is seeded per lattice cell, so each copy keeps its own size and tilt as the field drifts. Scatter pushes copies off their lattice point, up to about half a cell at 100%. Shuffle re-rolls the seed.

Snap holds the drift still between steps and then slides it across quickly. At 0 the movement is even.

Depth splits the copies into layers: the near ones are bigger and fall faster, the far ones smaller and hazier.

This stop

Drag the preview to place this stop; pinch, or scroll over it, to zoom. The transport steps between stops, and the play button lets the camera run the circuit.

Approach
Path between stops

Curved swings through each stop; straight corners at it. Ease decides how hard the camera slows into a stop — at 0 it travels at a constant speed. The circuit always returns to the first stop, so the loop still closes.

Sprite colour tints the built-in shapes. Your uploaded image keeps its own colours.

Background image

JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG, TIFF, and animated GIF or APNG. An animated background plays a whole number of times per loop, so the loop still closes seamlessly.

Background is chroma-key green (#00B140) and the gradient is off, in the preview and in exports. Avoid green sprites, or they will key out too.

Templates are a toggle. Loading one parks whatever you had on Yours; click the same template again, or Yours, to come straight back. Moving any control puts you back on Yours. A word you have typed or an image you have uploaded is kept — the template recolours it rather than replacing it.

How this works

Pattern Loop Generator tiles one sprite across a canvas and animates it, then encodes the result frame by frame into a video or an animated GIF. Every loop is built so the last frame runs back into the first without a jump.

Your files stay with you

Everything happens in this browser tab. Sprites and backgrounds you import are read straight from your device and never uploaded; the video and GIF are encoded on your own machine and saved by your browser. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Close the tab and nothing is kept beyond your choice of light or dark mode, which stays in this browser.

Working offline

Save the page to your device and it runs with no connection at all. The only thing it ever fetches is the two web fonts used by the interface and the text sprites; without them it falls back to the fonts you already have.

The camera

The Camera tab moves the frame over the pattern: a ring of stops, visited in order and then back to the first, so the move is a closed circuit like everything else here. Each stop holds a position, a zoom and a roll; the path between them curves through each stop or corners at it, and Ease decides how hard the camera slows as it arrives.

Keyboard

⇧T next template · ⇧B previous · ⇧R a random look · Space play or pause. Paste an image with ⌘V or Ctrl V to use it as a sprite.
